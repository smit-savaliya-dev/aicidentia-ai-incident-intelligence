# Key findings

_Generated by `aicidentia.models.story_outputs` from `reports/results.json` (AIID snapshot 20261026). Every number can be traced through its results.json key._

## 1. AI harm shifted from failures to misuse after ChatGPT

Malicious Actors & Misuse rose from 12% to 52% of MIT-labelled incidents after 30 Nov 2022, the largest shift in the risk-domain mix (Cramer's V 0.52; themes V 0.51).

- **Number:** 12% -> 52%
- **results.json:** `story.chatgpt_malicious_share_after`, `story.chatgpt_domains`
- **Limitation:** Reported incidents only; MIT coverage is lower after the cut-off (78% vs 98%) and AIID's editorial intake changed over the same years.
- **Dashboard page:** Themes and Trends

## 2. 5 of 12 risk themes are emerging

Deepfake videos and hoaxes, hallucinated legal citations, deepfake impersonation scams, AI nude images in schools and AI election disinformation all grow significantly as a share of incidents (Mann-Kendall with Benjamini-Hochberg, 2015-2025).

- **Number:** 5 emerging themes
- **results.json:** `rq1b.emerging_themes`, `rq1b.themes_share_trends`
- **Limitation:** Eleven complete years give little power; shares describe the database, not the real-world frequency of harm.
- **Dashboard page:** Themes and Trends

## 3. One in six recent incidents repeats a known failure, and search finds the precedent

17% of incidents since 2024 have an editor-linked earlier precedent, and searching only earlier incidents puts it in the top 5 for 76% of them; at depth 3 our search scores MRR 0.45 against 0.034 for AIID's built-in suggestions.

- **Number:** 76% found in top 5
- **results.json:** `rq4.repeat`, `rq4.mrr3`, `rq4.aiid_nlp_rr3`
- **Limitation:** Editors link only some incidents, so the share with a precedent is a lower bound and the search is scored on linked incidents only.
- **Dashboard page:** Precedent Finder

## 4. The AI-generated labels are usable but about 1 in 7 is doubtful

Editor-linked similar incidents share an MIT domain 75% of the time against 30% by chance, and the discrimination label agrees substantially with human CSETv1 annotation (kappa 0.74), yet confident learning estimates 15% of labels are wrong.

- **Number:** 15% estimated label noise
- **results.json:** `rq3rel.pairs_similar`, `rq3rel.mapping_kappa`, `rq3rel.cl_noise_rate`
- **Limitation:** Human fields map only partially onto MIT domains and cover pre-2024 incidents; confident learning inherits the classifier's own errors.
- **Dashboard page:** Label Reliability

## 5. Deepfake, scam and election harms concentrate around the big platforms

3 of the 5 emerging themes concentrate in network community 1, where 92%-96% of their incidents involve its entities (about 1.6 times the rate for all incidents), with Meta and YouTube as the most central allegedly involved organisations.

- **Number:** 96% of incidents
- **results.json:** `story.synthesis`, `rq2.community_profile`
- **Limitation:** Organisations are alleged deployers or developers, centrality reflects how often they are reported (not blame), and communities depend on a few hub entities.
- **Dashboard page:** Risk Network
